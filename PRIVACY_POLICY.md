# Privacy Policy — Veronica

**Last updated: June 3, 2026**

Veronica is a personal fitness coaching app that helps you track nutrition, steps, and workouts using AI-powered feedback. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

Veronica collects only what you provide directly or what your device sensors report:

- **Name** — optional, used to personalize Veronica's responses
- **Meal logs** — food names, calorie and macro estimates, photos you take or upload
- **Step count** — daily step totals read from your device's step counter sensor
- **Workout logs** — session names, exercises, sets, reps, and weights you record
- **Weight entries** — body weight readings you log manually
- **Goals and preferences** — calorie targets, step goals, fitness goal type, height, preferred tone, and language

No account, email address, or payment information is ever collected.

---

## 2. How Your Data Is Used

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features:

- Displaying your nutrition, step, and workout history
- Generating AI coaching messages, daily greetings, and weekly summaries via Veronica

---

## 3. Third-Party Services — Anthropic

To power Veronica's AI coaching, certain data is sent to **Anthropic, PBC** (makers of Claude) through their API:

- Food photos you submit for analysis
- Summaries of your recent nutrition, step count, workout, and weight data
- Your name and tone/language preferences (if set)

Anthropic processes this data to generate responses and is subject to their own [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy) and [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/usage-policy). No other third parties receive your data.

---

## 4. Data Storage

All your data — meals, workouts, weights, step history, and preferences — is stored **locally on your device** using Android's Room database and DataStore. No data is uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

Food photos are stored in your device's app cache and are not synced or backed up externally.

---

## 5. Permissions

Veronica requests the following device permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `CAMERA` | Taking food photos for meal analysis |
| `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION` | Reading your device's step counter sensor |
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` | Selecting food photos from your gallery |

Permissions are only used for the features described above and are not used for tracking or advertising.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can permanently delete all data at any time from **Settings → Reset All Data** inside the app. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Veronica is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
